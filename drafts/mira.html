Mira — logistics assistant

Draft — scripted conversation

Nothing here is connected to a model or to shipment data. It shows the interface and, more importantly, the boundary Mira must not cross.

M

I can help with services, coverage, documents and process. What are you shipping, and where from and to?

You

18 pallets of olive oil, Lattakia to Hamburg. How much and how long?

M

I can't give you a rate or a transit time — those depend on the sailing and the carrier's space, and they're confirmed by our operations team, not by me.

What I can tell you: 18 pallets at 120 × 80 × 145 cm is 25.06 CBM, which is 37% of a 40HC. Olive oil classifies under 1509.20, subject to the import authority's determination.

To get you a quotation I need the ready date and the incoterm. Shall I pass this to the team?
