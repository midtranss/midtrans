FAQ concept Readable support

Frequently asked questions

Answers grouped by real shipment questions

What details are needed for a fast quote?

Origin, destination, cargo description, packages, dimensions, gross weight, ready date and documents if available.

Can MIDTRANS review documents first?

Yes, document readiness should be reviewed before cargo moves where the route or customs exposure requires it.

Which freight modes are supported?

Sea, air, land, LCL, FCL, multimodal, customs clearance, warehousing and special cargo coordination.

Does MIDTRANS support Syria routes?

Yes, Syria-related routes are reviewed by cargo type, documents, route feasibility and destination access.
