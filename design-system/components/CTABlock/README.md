# CTABlock

The closing band of a public page: one action, stated plainly.

Compose `mt-cta` → `mt-cta__copy` (`mt-cta__title`, `mt-cta__text`) and `mt-cta__actions` (one `mt-btn--primary` and an optional `mt-cta__alt` link).

**You provide:** the copy per locale and the destinations.

It sits on `surface-inverse`, so the title is `ink-inverse` and the body is `ink-inverse-muted`. **One `surface-inverse` band per screen** — if the Footer is directly beneath, the CTA is the band and the footer keeps its own, which is why this block carries `space-10` of separation below it.

**The title is an instruction, not a question.** "Send your shipment details", not "Ready to ship?". The body says what the reader should send and what they get back.

**The primary button repeats the page's main action** — it is the same label as the hero's, in the same words, because a reader who scrolled past it once is looking for it again.

**Do not** put a form here — this block links to one. Do not use it mid-page as a divider, do not put two on a page, and never write an urgency claim ("limited space", "rates rising") on it.
