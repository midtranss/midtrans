## 1. Why this is the critical path

Phase 04 was chosen as the first content phase for one reason: Syrian trade procedure is the
subject where MIDTRANS has knowledge no competitor can replicate, accumulated since 1998.

That knowledge lives in people, not documents. **If these sessions do not happen, Phase 04
silently degrades into generic trade content** — which fails the uniqueness test in
`../standards/SEO-STANDARDS.md` §2, is worth nothing, and wastes the phase.

This is also why five knowledge-base entries were deliberately left unanswered rather than
drafted. They are waiting on these sessions.

## 2. Session design

| Item | Recommendation |
|---|---|
| Length | 60–90 minutes. Longer and recall degrades. |
| Participants | One interviewer, one or two operations staff who **do the work**, not who manage it |
| Recording | Audio-record with consent, and take notes. Transcribe afterwards. |
| Language | Whatever the interviewee is fluent in. Arabic for the Damascus and Dubai teams. |
| Interviewer | Does **not** need to be a writer. The job is to ask and capture, not to compose. |
| Follow-up | Expect a second short session per topic for gaps. Schedule it in advance. |

### The two rules that make a session work

**1. Ask for the exception, not the rule.** "What documents are needed" produces a list anyone
can find. "What makes a shipment sit at the port for a week" produces something only MIDTRANS
knows.

**2. Ask for a story, then extract the rule.** People recall specific shipments far better than
abstract procedure. *"Tell me about a shipment that went wrong recently"* yields more usable
detail than *"what problems occur?"*

## 4. Turning a session into content

Do not publish a transcript. Each session produces three distinct things:

| Output | Goes to | Shape |
|---|---|---|
| **Knowledge-base entry** | `mira/knowledge/kb-seed.yaml` | Short, direct, answers the question as asked |
| **Page content** | Phase 04 cluster | Long-form, structured, with the failure modes |
| **FAQ items** | `FAQPage` schema | Only genuine questions, per `SEO-STANDARDS.md` §4 |

### The originality test, applied at capture time

Before writing anything up, ask of each answer:

> **Could a competent competitor have written this?**

If yes, it is background — keep it for context, but it is not the content. The content is
whatever survives that question. Per `SEO-STANDARDS.md` §2, a page that does not survive it
should be merged or not published at all.

### Closing the loop

For every knowledge-base entry the session fills:

1. Write the answer in both EN and AR
2. Set `source` to the session and date
3. Set `owner` to the operations person who gave it — **by name**
4. Set `reviewed_at` to today, `expires_at` to 6 or 12 months out
5. Change `status` to `approved`
6. Run `python3 mira/knowledge/validate_kb.py` — it must pass before the entry is served

## 5. Handling what comes back

**If an answer includes a rate, a transit time or a cost** — and it will, because that is how
operations people think — record it for internal use if useful, but **it never enters the
knowledge base or a published page**. `validate_kb.py` will reject it mechanically, which is the
intended backstop.

**If two people give different answers**, that is a finding, not a problem. Record both and
escalate: a procedure two staff describe differently is one customers are getting inconsistently.

**If nobody knows**, write "unknown" and move on. An honest gap is a work item. A confident guess
is a future correction on a live page.

## 6. Risk

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| **Sessions do not happen** | This is the phase's whole premise. Schedule and confirm before Phase 04 starts, and treat a cancellation as a schedule slip, not a minor one. |
| Interviewee gives the textbook answer | Push for the exception and the story — questions 4–7 in each set exist for this |
| Knowledge stays with one person | Record the sessions. The point is that the knowledge outlives the individual. |
| Answers drift into rates and timings | Expected. Filter at write-up; `validate_kb.py` catches what slips. |
| Write-up loses the specifics | The specifics *are* the value. Generic phrasing at write-up undoes the whole session. |
