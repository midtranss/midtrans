## The two removals, in full

### 1. US state and city pages

**Removed:** Texas, Houston, California, Los Angeles, New York, New Jersey, Florida, Atlanta,
Chicago.

**Replaced by:** one page — *US companies entering Syria* (Phase 05 / D3).

**Reason:** templated location pages without unique operational substance are assessed at site
level. The exposure is not that the nine pages fail to rank — it is that a doorway-page pattern
can suppress the deep Syria content in Phase 04, which is the programme's most valuable asset.

Marginal upside; material risk to the whole domain. The original document asserted these were not
doorway pages, but an assertion in a plan provides no protection — only the pages' content does,
and there is no distinct operational reality for MIDTRANS in Atlanta versus Chicago.

**Reopening condition** (all three required) — see Phase 05:
1. Data shows genuine city-level search demand for this service
2. MIDTRANS has genuinely city-specific operational substance
3. Each page independently passes the uniqueness test

### 2. The 50-case-study target

**Reduced to:** 10 excellent case studies (Phase 07 / D1).

**Reason:** fifty thin case studies are fifty thin pages. Ten substantial ones carry more
commercial weight and pass the uniqueness test. The constraint is not ambition — it is that
genuine, permission-cleared case material is finite, and padding it produces exactly the content
this programme is structured to avoid.

## Additions that were not in the original roadmap

| Addition | Why |
|---|---|
| **Phase 00 — Diagnostics & Baseline** | The original contained no current-state data. Without a baseline, success is unmeasurable and the plan is unfalsifiable. |
| **`standards/MIRA-GUARDRAILS.md`** | The original instructed MIRA to be proactive with no constraint on quoting rates, transit times, costs, or acceptance. Direct commercial and legal exposure. |
| **`standards/LANGUAGE-SCOPE.md`** | The original never mentioned language. At 7 languages, a 1,500-page target is 10,500 URLs — the single most expensive omission available in a multilingual project. |
| **Phase exit gates** | The original had "Definition of Done" per phase but no metric gate. Delivering outputs is not the same as producing a result. |
| **Capacity estimates** | The original specified volume and quality with no statement of who would produce it. |
| **Risk and rollback sections** | Absent throughout the original. |

## What was retained unchanged

Three things from the original document were kept as they were, because they were its strongest
material:

1. **The writing standards and forbidden-word list** — now `standards/WRITING-STANDARDS.md`
2. **The specialist positioning** — Syria Trade + Maritime + Business Representation
3. **The revised sequencing conclusion** reached at the end of the original conversation:
   conversion before expansion, and *"the site already has good content but needs higher
   conversion more than more pages."*

That last conclusion is the foundation this entire programme is built on.
